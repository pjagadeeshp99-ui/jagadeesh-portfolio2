PROJECT 04 · GOOGLE WORKSPACE AUTOMATION

Invoice Management Dashboard

Automated Invoice Storage, Tracking & Retrieval System

Project Overview

Designed and developed a Google Sheets-based Invoice Management Dashboard to streamline invoice storage, tracking and retrieval. The system integrates Google Apps Script and Google Drive to automate PDF uploads, generate view and download links, and provide an interactive interface for managing invoice records.

Key Features

  • Invoice Upload Automation: Upload invoice PDFs directly through a custom HTML popup.
  • Duplicate Invoice Validation: Prevent duplicate invoice numbers.
  • Google Drive Integration: Automatically store invoice PDFs in a dedicated Drive folder.
  • Invoice Tracking Dashboard: Maintain invoice numbers, upload status and document access links.
  • View & Download: Provide direct PDF viewing and downloading functionality.
  • Invoice Search: Search and select invoice records through an interactive download interface.
  • Link Validation & Recovery: Check PDF availability and refresh document links.
  • Custom UI: Build custom HTML/CSS popups for invoice upload and retrieval.
  • Dashboard Formatting: Use structured headers, colour formatting, alignment and frozen rows.

Technology Stack

Google SheetsGoogle Apps ScriptGoogle DriveHTMLCSSJavaScript

My Role

Designed and developed the solution end-to-end, including dashboard structure, Apps Script backend, custom HTML interfaces, Google Drive integration, validation logic and invoice retrieval automation.

Outcome

Created a centralized invoice management system that simplifies invoice uploading, document access, tracking and retrieval while reducing manual file-management effort.
