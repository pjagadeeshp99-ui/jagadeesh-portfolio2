PROJECT 03 · IT ASSET MANAGEMENT

Asset Dashboard – IT Asset Management Web App

A centralized, Google Sheets-backed web application for IT asset inventory, physical verification, JumpCloud reconciliation and reporting.

Project Overview

Developed a web-based IT Asset Management dashboard to simplify physical verification, inventory tracking, discrepancy management and reporting through an automated, centralized system.

Role

Designed and developed end-to-end.

Key Features

  • Asset Dashboard: Live asset counts and centralized module navigation.
  • PV vs JumpCloud Reconciliation: Upload data, match Asset Tags and identify inventory discrepancies.
  • Discrepancy Management: Identify mismatches and maintain editable remarks.
  • Automated Reporting: One-click synchronization and dated report generation.
  • Laptop Inventory: Track working, prepared and non-usable laptops by model and location.
  • Accessories Management: Track accessory stock, utilization and compatible models.
  • Bulk Upload & Export: Excel import, duplicate detection, search and XLSX export.
  • Automated Setup: Automatically create required sheets, headers and formatting.
  • Live Integration: Google Sheets-backed data updates reflected in the dashboard.

Technology Stack

Google Apps Script (V8)Google SheetsHTMLCSSJavaScriptSheetJS

Highlights

  • Automated physical verification and JumpCloud reconciliation.
  • Centralized IT asset inventory management.
  • Automated reporting and synchronization.
  • Bulk Excel processing and duplicate prevention.
  • Google Sheets used as the central data source.
