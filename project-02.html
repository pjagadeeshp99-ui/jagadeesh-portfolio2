PROJECT 02 · GOOGLE APPS SCRIPT

Laptop Transit & Movement Automation

Automating laptop transit notifications and asset movement tracking between office locations.

Project Overview

Developed an automated laptop transit notification and movement tracking system to simplify the process of transferring IT assets between office locations.

Key Features

  • Select source asset data from Google Sheets.
  • Automatically retrieve laptop details using Asset Tags.
  • Schedule transit notification emails.
  • Configure recipients, CC, subject, and scheduled date.
  • Generate compact email tables containing Asset Tag, Serial Number, and Model.
  • Prevent duplicate email processing.
  • Maintain separate transit and inward/outward history records.
  • Track historical asset movements.

Technologies Used

Google Apps ScriptGoogle SheetsGmailJavaScriptAutomated Email Scheduling

Project Objective

Reduce manual communication during inter-office laptop transfers and maintain a structured record of asset movements.

Demo Environment: Sample office locations and fictional laptop inventory data.
