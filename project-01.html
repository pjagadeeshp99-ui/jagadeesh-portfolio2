PROJECT 01 · GOOGLE APPS SCRIPT

Dell Service Ticket & SLA Automation

Streamlining laptop repair complaint management through automated ticket tracking, service status updates, and SLA monitoring using Google Apps Script.

Project Overview

Developed an automated Dell service ticket tracking system using Google Apps Script, Gmail, and Google Sheets to simplify IT support ticket management and monitor SLAs.

Key Features

  • Extract Dell support case IDs from Gmail and identify service appointment and onsite service emails.
  • Automatically update ticket statuses: Pending, Dispatched, and Completed.
  • Track case IDs, serial numbers, and assigned IT technicians.
  • Calculate SLA working hours automatically.
  • Monitor SLA Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM, with warnings for approaching and breached SLAs.
  • Generate ticket reports and dashboard summaries.
  • Send email notifications for important ticket updates.

Technologies Used

Google Apps ScriptGoogle SheetsGmailJavaScriptTime-driven Triggers

Project Objective

Reduce manual ticket tracking, improve service visibility, and simplify SLA monitoring for IT support operations.

Demo Environment: Personal Gmail account with sample Dell-style ticket emails and dummy ticket data.
